## O*NET®

This package contains selected occupation codes/titles and a project crosswalk derived from the O*NET Database, U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. Source credit: **National Center for O*NET Development. (2026). O*NET 30.3 Database [Data set].** [Release archive](https://www.onetcenter.org/db_releases.html), [O*NET database license](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html), [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications by the project include selection of the instrument occupation domain, source-to-unit crosswalk organization, research/clinical component judgments and the combination rules. Project scores and inferred roles are authored operationalizations, not O*NET ratings or USDOL/ETA findings. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed or tested these modifications.

The retained local task statements match all 18,796 rows of official O*NET 30.3 by canonical content, and the retained task-rating text is byte-identical to official 30.3 (161,559 rows). They differ from 31.0. The 1,016 retained occupation code/title/description rows match both 30.3 and 31.0. Separately, the legacy candidate's 31.0 archive/Excel hashes match the official 31.0 release. This identifies retained content; it does not prove which task version the historical reviewers used when constructing their judgments. The lean scoring package contains no task narratives, descriptions, rating files or raw O*NET archives. All 419 crosswalk source code/title rows match both releases; 296 of the 303 instrument-unit codes/titles also match. Seven instrument units are project aggregation targets, not asserted individual O*NET occupations: 13-1020.00, 21-1018.00, 25-2052.00, 25-9045.00, 29-2010.00, 31-1120.00 and 53-1047.00. The historical judgment-source qualification remains.

## Other classification and construct sources

Occupation classification uses O*NET-SOC/SOC; [Bureau of Labor Statistics SOC documentation](https://www.bls.gov/soc/) explains classification by work performed. Industry codes/titles and establishment interpretation use the U.S. Census Bureau's [2022 NAICS](https://www.census.gov/naics/). The project selects 68 industries and authors research/clinical contexts; those contexts are not Census classifications of individual duties.

Research-role operationalization draws on OECD, *Frascati Manual 2015*, [doi:10.1787/9789264239012-en](https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264239012-en); clinical-role operationalization draws on the cited health-worker and occupation evidence. The package supplies project rules and scales, not copies of those source publications. Saved SDR development comparisons refer to NCSES Survey of Doctorate Recipients tabulations. They are criterion-exposed development checks, not independent individual-duty validation. Exact historical construct references and qualifications remain in the S1–S4 supplement, DATA_NOTES.md and deposited table catalog in the accompanying study-evidence archive in this same Zenodo record.
