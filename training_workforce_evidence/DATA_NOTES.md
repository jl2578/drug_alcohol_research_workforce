# Notes on the data tables

These notes explain how particular tables are built and what to watch for when using them. [DATA_DICTIONARY.csv](DATA_DICTIONARY.csv) defines each column, and [data/TABLE_INDEX.csv](data/TABLE_INDEX.csv) lists every table. The [supplement](supplement/Supplement_S1_S4.pdf), Sections S1–S4, gives the scientific methods and interpretation. The [table and figure catalog](TABLES.html) retains complete displays and their qualifications; the implementation notes below explain the supplied data and saved diagnostics. [REPRODUCTION.md](REPRODUCTION.md) distinguishes fresh fits from checks of saved results.


## Measurement and capture tables

[data/TABLE_INDEX.csv](data/TABLE_INDEX.csv) lists rows, columns and file names, which the Family column below uses. CSVs preserve source precision and blank cells. An empty estimate or interval is undefined/unavailable, never zero. Overlapping diagnostic tables must not be summed as independent samples.

| Family | Unit and base | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Human agreement tables (`human_*`) | Original-reader comparisons on 200 inclusion cases, nested 100 content cases, or the original random 50 detailed cases; unit pairs remain clustered within records | Reader A/B agreement before adjudication. `assigned_records` includes noncomparable cases. `agreement_n` and `comparable_n`/`jointly_coded_n` determine conditional agreement. Nominal kappa differs from exact agreement. |
| Human raw-unit tables | Source-aligned original one-to-one unit pairs in the random 50 | Split/merge/unmatched groups excluded from field comparisons and reported separately. Multiselect fields use exact sets and mean unit Jaccard, not a mean of record means. `contributing_GOID_n` is a record count, not an identifier. |
| Human attention diagnostic | Original 50 detailed records versus other 150 | Descriptive attention comparison, not a correction or causal effect. |
| Reference observability | Final adjudicated content reference, 100 cases per field | `coded`, `cannot_determine`, `not_applicable` and missing remain distinct. Uncertainty rates use coded-plus-cannot-determine bases. |
| Scope accuracy tables (`scope_*`) | Fixed 200 inclusion cases | Operational specificity counts all model non-inclusions among reference-out cases. Definite-out specificity requires model-out. Precision excludes reference-undetermined inclusions; bounds for such cases are not confidence intervals. |

### Measurement diagnostics

For a new licensed search/coding run, use the [reader package in this Zenodo record](README.md#separate-deposits), version 0.3.0-rc2: `search/topical_query.txt` holds the complete topical expression and `search/REVIEWER_SEARCH.md` gives the execution/export recipe. Section S1.1 records historical collection/roster construction; the current reader's new search-derived population need not match it. Preserve the distinction between publication-date filters and completion-year eligibility, and keep licensed XML, metadata and assignments in restricted storage.

Section S1.4 of the [supplement](supplement/Supplement_S1_S4.pdf) defines the reference samples and primary endpoints; the [measurement catalog](TABLES.html#dep1) supplies their full tables. Original reader-unit alignment used the described question, topic, purpose and supporting evidence, not local aim IDs or equal labels. Across 200 records, 203 one-to-one, three many-to-one, one one-to-many and two unmatched-B groups account for all 210 A and 212 B units. The random 50 yielded 49 one-to-one pairs on 48 records; three A and five B split/merge units remain outside conditional field comparisons. Supplemental or final-only targets do not enlarge original agreement.

In the raw B return, child fields had blanks: 25 each for stated purpose, unrelated therapeutic indication and substance labels, 48 for primary substance and 35 for assignment basis. These are raw blanks, not uncertainty or empty coded sets. A separate rule-fill diagnostic recorded 158 mechanical fills from unchanged original inputs. It neither replaces the raw evidence nor incorporates supplemented/consensus scientific answers. Unit Jaccard averages jointly coded matched units, not record means. Whole-record resampling retains dependent units; no finite-population correction is applied. The bootstrap uses a frozen Mersenne Twister stream and linear percentile limits. Paired labels, ordering and draws remain private.

Endpoint bases differ. Human binary scope uses 193 jointly definite pairs, excluding seven uncertainty pairs; focus categories use 199 pairs; domain agreement uses 112 jointly in-scope valid-domain pairs. General/unspecified domain is a valid residual category, not scope uncertainty. Conditional C8 exact-set agreement uses 78 nonempty set pairs; end-to-end exact correctness/Jaccard uses 79 reference-coded cases and treats the one model not-applicable response as a nonmatch/zero Jaccard. Order is excluded: six equal sets differ only in order. The twelve per-label rows overlap and include an unobserved label; perfect absence agreement does not establish detection ability.

Subgroup funding uses the fixed FY2005–2009 institutional baseline for both degrees, not S3's rolling five-/two-year windows. Denominators below 20 are flagged; empty unresolved groups are not estimable. The 41 discrepancy events concern 26 records, while 120 coded-value nonmatches, 25 applicability disagreements and 45 reference-uncertain cells are dependent field-level events, not independent-error denominators. Confidence cross-tabs and the random-50 attention comparison are descriptive, without confidence calibration or causal explanation.

## Degree and field composition (T20)

One row per adopted field family (including field-unavailable) by presentation credential group: 72 cells. All 13,227 distinct in-scope scientific works contribute exactly once. `distinct_works` is the integer cell count; `denominator_distinct_works` is always 13,227; `share_of_all_works_percent` is 100 times cell count divided by this denominator. `display_share_percent` is the publication label. Genuine zeros and positive shares below 0.1 percent remain distinct. Field and degree totals, with their full-corpus shares, are repeated on the corresponding rows for a self-contained numerical table; they must not be summed across repeated rows. Field definitions are the full adopted family names. `cell_status` distinguishes observed nonzero cells from genuine zeros. Reuse follows [DATA_AVAILABILITY.md](DATA_AVAILABILITY.md).

The formatted table is Table S2.1 in the supplement. The table describes all-corpus empirical composition, including groups without supported fitted profiles; it does not condition on content coding, institution linkage or model support and does not supply the equal-period weights used for the fitted profiles. Master’s credentials are pooled at the adopted master’s level; research doctorates follow the adopted PhD/documented-equivalent mapping; PsyD and DrPH remain separate; other determinate named doctorates are pooled. Other/unresolved credentials retain ambiguous/mixed/specialist states. Field-unavailable is separate from interdisciplinary/other.

## Research content implementation

Section S2 of the [supplement](supplement/Supplement_S1_S4.pdf) defines the estimands, support, resampling and held-out scores; [DEP2 in the catalog](TABLES.html#dep2) retains all results, unavailable states and figure captions. The original analysis used R 4.6.1 and brglm2 1.1.1 with mean-bias reduction, master seed 20261014, tolerance 1e−8 and 200 iterations. A predetermined same-draw retry allowed 1,000 iterations and half step at unchanged tolerance. Saturated group solutions are exact; institutional fits use a checked accelerated solution to the same adjusted score. These historical analysis requirements differ from the Python aggregate point-rebuild route in REPRODUCTION.

For repeated five-fold evaluation, sorted distinct work IDs were deterministically shuffled within institution-period strata and assigned round-robin. Each stratum retained training and evaluation representation. Saved splits, rather than equal-size assumptions, yield C3 training/evaluation ranges of 4,620–4,701/1,123–1,204 per fold, and C5/C4 ranges of 4,848–4,921/1,184–1,257. Across the folds, 5,824 C3 and 6,105 C5/C4 works are each evaluated once per repeat; complete split populations are in Table T19. IDs and assignments needed for a new fit remain private.

Differences pair endpoints by saved replicate ID. Missing endpoints make an attempt unavailable; marginal limits are not subtracted, unrelated draws are not paired and failed attempts are not replaced. The 10,000/8,000 group and 5,000/4,000 institution stream/prefix checks in S2 assess Monte Carlo stability, not interval coverage. All 5,280 pairwise contrasts have 10,000 usable attempts and pass inherited gates; the registry's 63 unavailable bounds remain unavailable. Diagnostic limits do not fill reportable blanks.

Fifteen inherited C3-coded works have C8 inapplicable (nine qualitative, five computational-modeling and one cross-sectional); four empirical/no-empirical-data flags and six data-type/unknown-source flags remain adjudication cases, not established errors. C3/C5 agreement does not validate C4/C8. Contributor deletion ranges are influence diagnostics, not new intervals: for earlier social-work/counseling master's portfolios, the largest institution supplies 37.1% of C4 and 40.6% of C8 eligible works, and the three largest 79.0% and 77.1%. The reviewed C4 organization/program degree contrast changes sign under some deletions and is not a broad degree claim.

## NIH funding tables

The annual panel covers FY2005–2023, NIDA and NIAAA actual funding contributions. The frozen official comparison on 29 September 2026 certified 69,382 contribution rows and $28,386,333,289 nominal dollars. A contribution is one application×fiscal year×actual funding IC. A search-export filename does not determine its funding IC. These tables retain no application, project, PI or person identifiers.

`institution_fy_funding.csv` contains 19,114 institution×FY rows: 976 direct source-owner or approved routing identities and 30 joint programs, each over 19 FYs. The direct universe comprises 921 approved institutional authorities, 53 observed source owners outside that authority roster, and two additional approved predecessor identities with no observed contributions. Those two are present only because approved exposure routes explicitly require their complete-source zero funding. They are not inferred from a failed lookup. Joint rows are approved annual partner unions and deliberately repeat partner exposure. They must not be added to direct rows to calculate source funding.

`nih_source_fy_accounting.csv` preserves complete source, assigned direct source, and unassigned source totals separately, as fiscal-year/component marginals. IC and mechanism categories are separate marginal families, not an IC-by-mechanism cross-tabulation. Assigned source totals are 53,837 contribution rows and $19,590,110,716. Unassigned source totals are 15,545 rows and $8,796,222,573. Unassigned source funding is not recoded as a zero institution or assigned to an invented institutional owner. The source accounting table is the appropriate source-conservation denominator. Direct-source nominal dollars, contribution counts and annual distinct application counts reconcile exactly to assigned source accounting. Rounded real-dollar subtotals can differ by cents when added; nominal amounts and the supplied BRDPI support unrounded recomputation.

`funding_exposure_institutions.csv` contains the 951 approved exposure institutions, including 30 joint programs. The exposure file is the complete 951-institution × 14-completion-year × 2-degree grid, or 26,628 rows. In isolation this NIH grid carries no observed dissertation-cell flag. The jointly released RQ3 panel identifies observed retained model cells through the same keys, so the complete funding grid is not a joint-release membership protection. The hypothetical completion years are 2010–2023. Master's exposures use FY t−2 through t−1. Doctoral exposures, including professional doctorates, use FY t−5 through t−1. Degree level controls only the funding window. This dataset contains no observed degree output, research-degree classification, model eligibility, outcomes or dissertation counts.

The annual and exposure files distinguish positive funding, certified zero funding, and unresolved ownership. Complete global source coverage and approved ownership/routing support explicit zeros. Mayo ownership remains unresolved: 19 annual rows and 28 complete-grid window rows have blank numeric funding metrics. Blank is unavailable, not zero. A missing row outside the declared universe says nothing about funding.

`funding_ownership_routes.csv` records the funding rows selected for every hypothetical completion year and each of its prior five FYs. Master's windows use the last two of those FYs. The routes preserve contemporaneous ownership, approved joint annual partner unions, and seven approved predecessor/successor cases. Predecessor funding is reused only for affected successor completion windows. The Toledo FY2007 exception is retained. Joint and predecessor reuse are exposure definitions, not additional source dollars. These rules are author decisions and documented institutional history; NIH does not assign dissertations to institutions.

`F_combined` is the distinct application count within each FY after pooling NIDA/NIAAA; window values sum those annual counts. It is not a distinct-project or distinct-award count over an entire window. `F_NIDA` and `F_NIAAA` can overlap. `F_both_IC` records their annual overlap, so the combined count equals NIDA plus NIAAA minus both. `contributions_...` counts actual IC contribution rows. Dollars sum actual IC costs, without duplicating a contribution across funding institutes.

The 104 observed activity codes are partitioned by the supplied author-defined mechanism map into research, training/education/fellowship, career development, excluded intramural, excluded contract and excluded other. The excluded groups remain in combined/source accounting. Treating research, training and career as an exhaustive partition without the excluded groups would fail conservation. NIH activity-code documentation provides mechanism context; the grouping is a study definition.

Real dollars use NIH's FY26 budget March 2025 BRDPI table, rebased to 2023=100. For each selected fiscal year, convert nominal dollars by multiplying by 100 and dividing by that FY's index. Sum with Decimal precision 50, then round each final component/window half up to cents. To reconstruct windows, use annual nominal dollars and the price index. Summing annual values already rounded to cents can differ from rounding the window once.

The rebuild script in the ZIP reconstructs window counts by adding selected owner-FY counts. A private audit of the entire pinned source found zero application/FY identities assigned to multiple direct source owners, so this arithmetic is exact for these routes and this snapshot. All existing multiowner union routes were also checked against the adopted union outputs. New source snapshots or changed ownership decisions must repeat that audit or supply approved union aggregates. The pinned snapshot also has zero application/FY identities crossing assigned and unassigned source categories, verified before asserting the count partition. No claim of universally additive application counts is made.

These tables cannot reconstruct application-level ownership decisions or model sample membership from its sanitized aggregates, and cannot refit outcome models without separately permitted outcome, denominator and analysis design inputs.

`nih/provenance/funding_sources.csv` in [data/nih_funding_rebuild.zip](data/nih_funding_rebuild.zip) lists logical sources, versions, official URLs where applicable, SHA-256 hashes, and handling. [DATA_DICTIONARY.csv](DATA_DICTIONARY.csv) documents each funding table column. Institutional identity uses a common minted key across the package; the private original-to-minted bridge is excluded.

## NIH-institution crosswalk

`nih_recipient_institution_crosswalk.csv` is a projection of the current adjudicated, officially certified NIH contribution authority at **recipient IPF × exact fiscal year × actual funding IC**. It covers observed FY2005–2023 contributions from NIDA and NIAAA. It contains 12,757 distinct mapping rows: 7,158 with a selected owner, 5,598 left unassigned/outside the current authority, and one explicitly conditional historical assignment. It does not claim to map every NIH recipient, all NIH funding, unobserved years or every award associated with a recipient. A row's fiscal year is its exact temporal applicability; do not fill missing years between observed rows.

The historical FY2013 IPF578422 decision applies to a particular application. Its public row deliberately has no selected `institution_id`, retains its candidate institutional key and requires the private application scope. The ledger's contemporary UNITID is context, not an instruction to fill the blank owner. Current evidence has no multiple-owner recipient-FY-IC group, but the schema and builder preserve such ambiguity if one arises. Blank recipient IPFs are omitted rather than fabricated. Signed recipient-identifier tokens are preserved exactly as certified in the source, including negative tokens; they are not converted to absolute values or assumed to be ordinary positive IPFs. Unassigned recipient rows are not zero-funding institutions.

The crosswalk is a **direct recipient-owner mapping**. It does not redirect historical predecessors globally or allocate their funding to all successors. Funding-window routing and joint-partner pooling belong in the separate funding tables. In particular, a joint analytical unit is not an additional NIH recipient, and its pooled exposure must not be summed with direct-owner source funding.

`analysis_institution_identity.csv` supplies a shared key space for the funding tables. Its 1,142 rows include the 951 approved funding-frame identities plus institutional owners needed for complete NIH source accounting, joint partners and explicit predecessor references. Registry membership does not assert an observed dissertation, an award or a nonzero outcome. `universe_role` explains the coverage. The authored `INST` keys are stable within this version; they are neither global institutional identifiers nor an anonymity claim. Their bridge to internal source keys is not shared. Internal keys can embed proprietary school codes, so none are exported.

Canonical names and state abbreviations are projected **only** from the official IPEDS HD directories for 2010–2023, using the latest directory containing each UNITID and recording its year. They are identity labels, not legal-name assertions for every historical fiscal year. Joint and non-IPEDS source labels are omitted. A valid official UNITID may lack a name because it does not occur in those available directory years. All other IPEDS columns, including Carnegie classifications and staff/person fields, are omitted.

The exact input-byte hashes appear in `nih/provenance/crosswalk_logical_sources.csv` in the ZIP, with logical source roles and official source URLs. Source-hash provenance is evidence of the frozen inputs, not evidence that every raw input can be redistributed. Public input information: [NIH RePORTER](https://reporter.nih.gov/), [NIH RePORTER API](https://api.reporter.nih.gov/?urls.primaryName=V2.0), and [IPEDS downloads](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/use-the-data/download-access-database). These references identify upstream sources; they do not claim a fresh download on the preparation date. The certified NIH official comparison was recorded on 29 September 2026.

These tables contain institutional administrative facts and authored institutional decisions; that is distinct from person-to-dissertation linkage. NIH and IPEDS attribution does not establish rights to any ProQuest or Carnegie-derived material; neither is copied into this institutional projection.

### Funding and investigator diagnostics

Historical [Carnegie Basic Classifications](https://carnegieclassifications.acenet.edu/carnegie-classification/resources/) context used the edition after its nominal year: 2010 completions use 2005; 2011–2015 use 2010; 2016–2018 use 2015; 2019–2021 use 2018; and 2022–2023 use 2021. This is a study assignment convention, not publication-date evidence. Unknown/conflicting values remain unknown. Carnegie context is not a covariate in the institutional models or a column in the public institutional projection.

Section S3.1 specifies the adopted institution-count inference. The saved category-count and portfolio-rate families each contain 132 targets per ownership population; selective checks contain eighteen per population, including unavailable focus targets. Neither family inherits the six original count targets' uncertainty policy. Institutional content has 488 available points and 40 unavailable states. Portfolio-rate PPML is unbounded: its maximum at observed funding is 1.461; one sparse doctorate single-case model has endpoint extrapolations of 782.928 at one annualized project and 57.491 at four. These are mean/support diagnostics, not probabilities. No clipping, renormalization or replacement model is adopted. The [selective-check table](TABLES.html#dep3-e03) preserves all targets and the distinction between unavailable primary inference and nonadopted method-transfer intervals.

Original investigator calibration held predictors and linkage networks fixed and examined independent Poisson outcomes, moderate/strong mean-preserving network and serial-PI shocks, and broad-network dependence under null/fitted slopes. Earlier target-specific groupings used 500 simulated datasets per scenario/truth cell; joint-degree groupings used 300. These are distinct diagnostic designs, not additional observed samples. Retained common support was iterated in simulated fits. The adopted broad-network full-refit design and its examined coverage are stated in S3.3. The 1,309 original leave-one-group-out refits retain aggregate success counts, standard errors and ranges; identities are excluded. Score-bootstrap companions used 19,999 Rademacher draws per target/grouping and lack the adopted corrected-t calculation's complete nonlinear coverage calibration. Their supplied inversions/limits do not replace adopted inference; only the three-target p adjustment is recomputed publicly.

Opioid tables retain two different fitted-value definitions (literal negative and below −1e−10), and distinguish the full descriptive pair from adjustment-eligible samples.
