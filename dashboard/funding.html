How do NIH drug and alcohol funding connections relate to training?

A work had an identified NIDA/NIAAA funding connection if its dissertation or thesis advisor or a committee member was a principal investigator on a NIDA- or NIAAA-funded project before the work was completed, if its author held a linked NIDA/NIAAA fellowship or dissertation grant (F30, F31 or R36), or both.
