Who trains in U.S. drug and alcohol research, what they study, and where they go

13,227 in-scope dissertations and theses completed 2010–2023, linked to NIH drug and alcohol funding and, for a sample of authors, to later employment.

In scope means at least one research aim, judged from the title and abstract, substantively concerns drugs or alcohol, the substances studied by NIDA and NIAAA, through their use, addiction, exposure or pharmacology, substance-related care, services, policy or education, or research on the body’s own related systems.
