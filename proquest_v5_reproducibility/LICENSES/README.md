Credit the Training-to-workforce contributors and the applicable publication or release citation; identify changes to CC BY material.

These grants cover rights held by the project authors. Third-party material retains its own terms, and public-domain facts are not assigned new copyright restrictions. Protected Carnegie material with noncommercial/sharealike conditions is excluded from the permissively licensed bundle. Source-constrained ProQuest materials and derived-data files are not assigned an unrestricted open license by this policy.

The current reviewer package distributes original methods, code and instructions, with no source records or prebuilt study datasets. Each reader obtains licensed inputs through their own authorized access. Restricted inputs and identifying research records remain outside the public package; access rights are not granted by either license.
